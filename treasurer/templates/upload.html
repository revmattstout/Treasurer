How online giving is counted

Gifts are counted once, from the giving records (gross amount, by fund). When the platform later deposits a lump-sum payout in the bank, that deposit is categorized Online Giving Payout and isn't counted as income a second time. Platform fees appear under Bank & Processing Fees. If you only upload bank statements, categorize those payouts as Donations – Cash & Check (or another income category) instead.
